❌ WRONG!

The correct answer is Diabetes.

The fruit of Coccinia grandis (L.) Voigt. is traditionally believed to be used as a remedy for diabetes.

🌿 INTERESTING FACT

DID YOU KNOW?

The young fruit of Coccinia grandis can be eaten as a vegetable or ulam. It can be blanched, eaten fresh, or added to curry and soup.
