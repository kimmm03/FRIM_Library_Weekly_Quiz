LIBRARY WEEKLY QUIZ

Week 01 - Question 03

🎉 CORRECT!

The leaves of Eugeissona tristis Griff., also known as bertam, can be used to make roofing.

Bertam is a wild palm commonly found in the forests of Malaysia and Thailand. Its leaves can also be used for various traditional purposes.

🌿 INTERESTING FACT

DID YOU KNOW?

The skin of the bertam leaf stalk can be used to make kelarai, which can be used for walls or ceilings. It can also be made into blinds to block sunlight.
