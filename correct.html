Week 01

🎉 CORRECT!

In Thailand, Clerodendrum paniculatum L. has traditionally been used to alleviate fever and treat inflammation.

Traditional uses of this plant have been reported across several Asian countries, including Malaysia, India, China, Korea and Japan.

🌿 INTERESTING FACT

DID YOU KNOW?

In Malaysia, the infusion has traditionally been applied externally for a distended stomach and taken as a purgative.
